[size=5][b]RDR2 DSX Rumble[/b][/size] - v1.6
[i]Context-aware rumble, adaptive triggers and lightbar for the DualSense, through DSX.[/i]

[b]First of all, if you find a bug and/or want something added please leave a comment and I will do that.[/b]

[size=4][b]What's new[/b][/size]
[b]v1.6[/b]
[list]
[*][b]In-game settings menu:[/b] press [b]F11[/b] to change rumble strength, footsteps, horse, guns, fistfights, the response curve, adaptive triggers, lightbar and more. It works with the mouse, the keyboard or the controller, pauses the game while it is open, and saves straight to the ini. There is also a "Test rumble" button.
[*][b]Update notice:[/b] when you load in, a short card tells you whether the mod is up to date or a new version is out. It checks a tiny version file on GitHub once per start; turn it off with [i]CheckForUpdates=0[/i] or in the menu.
[*][b]Rumble in cutscenes:[/b] footsteps, hoofbeats, your own shots and the gunfire around you now rumble during cutscenes too. You can switch it off in the F11 menu or with [i]CutsceneRumble=0[/i].
[/list]

Red Dead Redemption 2 on PC gives the DualSense only basic vibration. This mod reads what is happening in the game and turns it into rumble that makes sense: every footstep, every hoofbeat in the horse's gait, a kick for each shot that matches the gun's calibre, and adaptive triggers modelled on the real weapons.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[b]Rumble[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Footsteps[/b] - one pulse per real step, taken from the character's foot bones (not a timer). Walking is light, sprinting is a clear tap.
[*][b]Horse[/b] - hoofbeats that follow the gait: walk (4-beat), trot (2-beat), canter (3-beat), gallop (rolling 4-beat). Also landings after jumps, a kick when you spur, and a jolt when the horse is hit.
[*][b]Gunshots[/b] - every shot is a crisp crack plus a recoil kick, sized to the gun: the .22 Varmint is a tap, revolvers and repeaters a firm kick, rifles stronger, and shotguns and the Elephant Rifle hit hardest. The Schofield and LeMat kick harder than the Cattleman.
[*][b]Reloading[/b] - a short mechanical click.
[*][b]Weapon switching[/b] - a crisp detent click when you draw or switch a weapon, a softer one when you holster.
[*][b]Damage and death[/b] - a short impact plus a body hit that scales with the damage taken.
[*][b]Fistfights[/b] - a hard, short smack when you take a punch, and a firm knuckle thud when yours lands (heavier on a knockout).
[*][b]Water[/b] - wading on foot or on horseback gives a soft, swelling drag that grows with depth. Rivers and creeks push against you even when you stand still; still water only stirs when you move. A splash when you jump in.
[*][b]Swimming[/b] - soft, rhythmic strokes that quicken when you swim faster, and a faint muffled hum underwater.
[*][b]Animal attacks[/b] - a dog or wolf bite clamps down and then tugs as it shakes its head; a bear or cougar mauls with a crushing blow; a bull or bison rams with a deep, blunt hit. A low, swelling growl when a bear turns on you and roars.
[*][b]Doors[/b] - a crisp bang when a door next to you slams into its frame.
[*][b]Carrying bodies[/b] - a strained heave as you lift a body onto your shoulder, and a dull thud when you set it down.
[*][b]Sliding[/b] - a rough, gravelly scrape while you slide down a slope.
[*][b]Falls and landings[/b] - from a light bump to a heavy slam, depending on how far you fell. On horseback, drops off ledges and landings after jumps, plus a hard crash when the horse runs into a tree or rock.
[*][b]Wagons, coaches and trains[/b] - barely felt at a crawl, a hard rattle with frequent bumps at full speed.
[*][b]Cutscenes[/b] - the same footsteps, hoofbeats and shots while a cutscene plays, plus the gunfire of everyone nearby (a sharp crack up close, a dull thump further away). Optional.
[*][b]Low health[/b] - a heartbeat (lub-dub) that speeds up as health drops.
[*][b]Menus[/b] - light clicks on button presses in the pause menu, map, journal, catalogs, shops and the start menu.
[*][b]Startup splash[/b] - a kick for the gunshot in the startup splash, detected from the game's audio.
[/list]

[b]Adaptive triggers (through DSX)[/b]
[list]
[*][b]R2[/b] - a trigger "wall" that breaks like a real sear, tuned per weapon. Light pistols are light and short, single-action revolvers short and crisp, the Double-Action has a long, heavy pull, rifles are firmer, and double-barrel shotguns and the Elephant Rifle are the stiffest.
[*][b]L2[/b] - light aiming resistance that matches the weight of the gun.
[*][b]Bow[/b] - resistance builds as you draw and snaps back on release (the Improved Bow is stiffer).
[*][b]Lasso[/b] - rope tension.
[*][b]Weapon wheel[/b] - both triggers go loose while L1 is held, so nothing feels odd while you choose.
[*][b]Horseback[/b] - a galloping effect in the triggers while cantering or galloping unarmed (optional).
[*]Triggers go loose while reloading, in menus and when dead.
[/list]

[b]Lightbar[/b]
[list]
[*]The colour follows your health (green, yellow, red), flashes red when you are hit and pulses at low health.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/]Script Hook RDR2[/url] by Alexander Blade (includes the ASI loader, [i]dinput8.dll[/i])
[*][url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1812620/DSX/]DSX[/url] (Steam, v3) with the DualSense connected
[*]A DualSense controller
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]Script Hook RDR2[/b]: copy [i]ScriptHookRDR2.dll[/i] and [i]dinput8.dll[/i] into the game folder (the folder with [i]RDR2.exe[/i]).
[*]Copy [b]RDR2DSXRumble.asi[/b] and [b]RDR2DSXRumble.ini[/b] into the same game folder.
[*]Set up DSX:
[list]
[*][b]Settings > Networking:[/b] turn on [b]Incoming UDP[/b] (default address 127.0.0.1, port 6969).
[*]Leave the controller emulation as DSX sets it up (virtual DualShock 4 or Xbox both work).
[*][b]Haptics/Rumble:[/b] turn [b]Rumble to Haptics OFF[/b]. With it on, all rumble feels very weak. Leave [b]Enhanced Rumble Experience ON[/b].
[/list]
[*]Start DSX first, then the game.
[/list]

The game folder should then contain:
[code]
Red Dead Redemption 2\
  RDR2.exe
  dinput8.dll
  ScriptHookRDR2.dll
  RDR2DSXRumble.asi
  RDR2DSXRumble.ini
[/code]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
Press [b]F11[/b] in game for the settings menu (mouse, keyboard or controller: D-pad, Cross, Circle). Everything else is in [b]RDR2DSXRumble.ini[/b]; changes there apply [b]while the game is running[/b] (within about 2 seconds).
[list]
[*][b]Intensity[/b] - overall strength in % (0-200).
[*][b]ResponseCurve[/b] - how far soft effects are pushed down compared to strong ones (1.0 = linear, 1.5 recommended, 2.0 = bigger gap).
[*][b]Footsteps, Horse, Weapons, Reload, WeaponSwitch, Damage, Melee, Water, Swimming, Animals, Doors, Carry, Sliding, Landing, Vehicle, Heartbeat[/b] - each effect in %, 0 = off.
[*][b][Menu] ButtonRumble / Intensity[/b] - menu clicks.
[*][b][Startup] SplashShotRumble / ShotThreshold[/b] - the splash shot. Lower ShotThreshold if it doesn't trigger.
[*][b][DSX] AdaptiveTriggers, GallopingTriggersOnHorse, Lightbar[/b] - switch the DSX features on or off.
[*][b]OnlyWithController[/b] - no rumble while you play with keyboard and mouse.
[*][b]MuteWhenUnfocused[/b] - rumble stops as soon as you alt-tab out of the game.
[*][b]CutsceneRumble[/b] - rumble during cutscenes (1 = on, 0 = off).
[*][b]MenuPausesGame[/b] - the game pauses while the F11 menu is open.
[*][b]CheckForUpdates[/b] - the startup notice that tells you if a new version is out (reads one small file from GitHub, nothing is sent).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Other vibration or DualSense mods[/b] (for example Vibration Expanded or other DualSense trigger/haptics mods) will double the rumble or fight over the triggers. Disable them, for example by renaming their [i].asi[/i] to [i].asi.off[/i]. To keep another mod's triggers, set [i]AdaptiveTriggers=0[/i].
[*]Works alongside trainers such as Native Trainer.
[*]Story mode only. Do not use ASI mods in Red Dead Online.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]No rumble at all:[/b] check that DSX is running and Incoming UDP is on. Look in [i]RDR2DSXRumble.log[/i] in the game folder: it should show "pad: virtual DualShock 4" (or an XInput line) and "script started".
[*][b]Rumble is very weak:[/b] turn off "Rumble to Haptics" in DSX and set DSX's rumble intensity to maximum.
[*][b]Game shows "Fatal: can't find native":[/b] update Script Hook RDR2 to the latest version for your game version.
[*][b]Adaptive triggers do nothing:[/b] Incoming UDP in DSX is off or on a different port. Set the port with [i]Port=[/i] in the ini.
[*][b]Footsteps too strong or too weak:[/b] adjust [i]Footsteps[/i] or [i]ResponseCurve[/i].
[/list]

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete [i]RDR2DSXRumble.asi[/i], [i]RDR2DSXRumble.ini[/i] and [i]RDR2DSXRumble.log[/i] from the game folder.
