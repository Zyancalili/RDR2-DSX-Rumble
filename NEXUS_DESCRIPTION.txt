[size=5][b]RDR2 Controller Rumble[/b][/size] - v1.6
[i]Rumble that follows what happens in the game - for DualSense and Xbox controllers. (Formerly "RDR2 DSX Rumble".)[/i]

[b]DualSense users: [url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1812620/DSX/]DSX[/url] is required.[/b] It's the best way to play: you also get adaptive triggers and the lightbar. Xbox controllers work without anything extra.

[b]First of all, if you find a bug and/or want something added please leave a comment and I will do that.[/b]

[size=4][b]What's new in v1.6[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Xbox controller support (rumble only, no DSX needed) - hence the new name
[*]F11 in-game settings menu (mouse, keyboard or controller)
[*]Heavier hoofbeats and drag in deep snow
[*]Optional rumble in cutscenes
[*]Startup notice when a new version is out
[/list]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Footsteps[/b] - one tap per real step
[*][b]Horse[/b] - hoofbeats that follow the gait, jumps, crashes, deep snow
[*][b]Guns[/b] - a kick sized to each weapon, reload and weapon-switch clicks
[*][b]Fights and damage[/b] - punches given and taken, wounds, animal attacks
[*][b]World[/b] - water, swimming, doors, carrying bodies, sliding, falls, wagons, cutscenes
[*][b]Low health[/b] - a heartbeat
[*][b]DualSense only:[/b] adaptive triggers tuned per weapon (plus bow and lasso), lightbar that follows your health
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.dev-c.com/rdr2/scripthookrdr2/]Script Hook RDR2[/url]
[*]DualSense + [url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1812620/DSX/]DSX[/url] (recommended), or an Xbox controller
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install Script Hook RDR2.
[*]Copy [i]RDR2DSXRumble.asi[/i] and [i]RDR2DSXRumble.ini[/i] into the game folder (next to [i]RDR2.exe[/i]).
[*]DualSense only: in DSX turn on [b]Incoming UDP[/b] (Settings > Networking) and turn [b]Rumble to Haptics OFF[/b]. Start DSX before the game.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
Press [b]F11[/b] in game. Everything else is in [i]RDR2DSXRumble.ini[/i] (changes apply while the game runs).

[size=4][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]No rumble:[/b] DualSense - check DSX is running with Incoming UDP on. Then check [i]RDR2DSXRumble.log[/i] in the game folder.
[*][b]Weak rumble on DualSense:[/b] turn off "Rumble to Haptics" in DSX.
[*][b]Double rumble or fighting triggers:[/b] disable other vibration/DualSense mods.
[*]Story mode only.
[/list]
